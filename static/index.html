ElderCare Vision Offline demo

AI Summit demo · privacy-first video understanding

Know their day went well, without watching hours of video.

Each clip is analysed on our own server. A small pose model finds people and how they are positioned, simple rules you can read spot falls, long lying and time in each room, and a language model writes a short note for family and caregivers.

  1. Pose17 body points per person, six times a second, on CPU.
  2. RulesFalls, lying outside bed or sofa, long stillness, time per room.
  3. SummaryA plain-language daily note with things to check.
